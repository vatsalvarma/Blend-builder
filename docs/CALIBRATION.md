# Calibration Session Recipe

1. **Roast**: Light roast each bean to standard sample roast specs.
2. **Rest**: Let beans rest for 8-24 hours.
3. **Brew**: Standard cupping protocol (8.25 g coarse grind per 150 ml, water at ~93°C, 4 minutes steep).
4. **Process**: Break the crust, skim, and slurp at hot, warm, and cool temperatures.
5. **Panel**: Use 2-3 blind-coded tasters.
6. **Anchors**: Use two anchor cups every session (e.g., Monsooned Malabar for Tangy = 1, and the brightest bean for Tangy = 9).
7. **Score**: Score the 8 primary axes (Fruity, Flowery, Sweet, Nutty, Spicy, Tangy, Strong, Bitter) from 0 to 10 over three sessions and average to the nearest 0.5.
8. **Storage**: The stored `Bitter` value must be the tasted `Bitter` value divided by `0.7` (because the Bitter roast multiplier starts at 0.7 for light roasts).

*Starter sheet beans recommended: Plantation Arabica, Robusta, Monsooned Malabar, Liberica, Monkey Parchment.*
*Note: Wait for 30+ cafe verdicts before changing a formula in production.*
